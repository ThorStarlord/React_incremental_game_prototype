# GC-07 Chapter 5 Preregistration — The Chrono-Crypt

**Status:** IMPLEMENTATION PREREGISTRATION  
**Program:** Campaign One / 1.0 Game Completion  
**Prepared:** 2026-09-20

## Dramatic function

Chapter 4 proves that local stabilization works but is not a countermeasure. Chapter 5 returns Lyra and the player to the Chrono-Crypt for a new purpose: derive a reusable counterphase principle from older harmonic failures.

The earlier `quest_lyra_chrono_crypt_calibration` remains Chapter 3 relationship history. GC-07 does not relabel or replace it.

## Entry

- `lyra_gc06_exp_chrono_crypt_route` exists;
- the Merchant District lattice was stabilized before that evidence could be recorded;
- Lyra is already a calibrated adversarial/cooperative counterpart.

## Authored travel

Add `location_chrono_crypt` as an authored travel node connected to Whispering Woods.

Travel access is gated by existing Relationship evidence:

```text
lyra_gc06_exp_chrono_crypt_route
```

Adjacency alone is insufficient. No generic key-item or world-level unlock subsystem is introduced.

## Active problem

Main-story Quest: `quest_gc07_counterphase_principle`.

Objective:

```text
reach location_chrono_crypt
-> inspect the harmonic failure chamber
-> choose one legal derivation approach
```

## Legal approaches

1. **Manual Harmonic Triangulation** — baseline; no optional Trait required.
2. **Structural Counterphase** — requires `WillowsWisdom + ConstraintSense`.
3. **Adversarial Countermodel** — requires `ScholarlyInsight + AdversarialCalibration`.

The capability routes deepen the two GC-04 build profiles without creating a build-profile subsystem.

## Durable consequence

Each Quest route records route-specific Lyra Relationship evidence.

After a legal route, a Lyra interpretation dialogue records:

- `fact_gc07_counterphase_principle` in NPC Knowledge;
- `lyra_gc07_exp_counterphase_derived`;
- a player-visible durable Memory explaining that counterphase is a reusable principle rather than a one-off local fix.

## Exit

Chapter 5 completes only when:

- one legal crypt derivation route exists in Relationship history;
- the counterphase interpretation Experience exists;
- the durable Memory exists;
- Lyra knows the counterphase principle.

Those authorities are sufficient for Chapter 6 network preparation.

## Rejection / negative paths

- travel to the Chrono-Crypt rejects before GC-06 exit evidence;
- direct non-adjacent travel still rejects through M18 adjacency authority;
- partial Trait pairs do not unlock a profile route;
- baseline remains legal without optional Traits;
- counterphase interpretation rejects before a legal crypt route;
- no generalized time-dilation state, timer, readiness currency, ChapterEngine, or narrative DSL;
- no Copy may solve or commit the crypt decision.

## Persistence

Location, Quest, Relationship, Memory, Knowledge, and permanent Traits already persist. GC-07 adds no chapter-owned save root.
