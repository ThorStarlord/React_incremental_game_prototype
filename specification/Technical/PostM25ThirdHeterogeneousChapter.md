# Post-M25 Third Heterogeneous Chapter

**Status:** REPOSITORY-ONLY / HERMETIC QUALIFICATION  
**Human product-quality evidence:** UNPROVEN  
**New chapter runtime authority:** None

## Purpose

Exercise the post-Rule-of-Two chapter projection with a third structurally different existing content arc before considering broader chapter infrastructure.

The third chapter is **Enemies in Phase**, projected from the already-authored Lyra relationship arc.

```text
strategic defeat
-> coercion reflected
-> reluctant co-training
-> ideological friction
-> mutual calibration
-> enemies in phase
```

Unlike Merchant District Crisis and Archive Inquiry, this chapter deliberately has one completion route. Its heterogeneity lies in adversarial learning and cooperation without ideological convergence, not in branching for its own sake.

## Authority model

Completion is derived only from canonical Relationship Experiences already owned by the Lyra runtime. The existing `lyra_memory_enemies_in_phase` remains the durable relationship-history evidence. The chapter projection creates no new memory, reducer, completion flag, save field, or state machine.

## Result

Three chapter-scale shapes now coexist:

- Merchant District Crisis — institutional/world-state composition with two conclusions;
- Archive Inquiry — evidence/model-revision composition with two distinct relationship histories;
- Enemies in Phase — linear adversarial-calibration composition with one route.

This increases evidence that chapter-scale content can remain a projection over domain authorities while content shapes vary.

## Evidence ceiling

The qualification proves deterministic derivation from existing Lyra evidence. It does not prove that humans experience this sequence as a satisfying chapter, understand the adversarial bond, enjoy its pacing, or prefer this structure.
