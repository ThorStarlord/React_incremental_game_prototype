## Decision

For bounded product-directed development, select this provisional hierarchy:

1. **Primary promise — Relationship-Derived Capability Buildcraft.** Who the player learns from, and what happened with them, becomes part of the player's durable capability identity.
2. **Supporting identity — Causal Legibility.** Already-earned canonical evidence should explain why a capability, opportunity, consequence, or delegation option exists without exposing hidden future content.
3. **Incremental identity — Earned Delegation / Mastery Compression.** Repeatable work becomes delegatable only after the player has personally learned it and existing Copy eligibility permits it.
4. **Enabling architecture — Heterogeneous Composition.** Varied authored content continues to compose over existing domain authorities without a generalized chapter engine unless repeated friction proves one necessary.

## Product loop

```text
meaningful relationship / world event
-> player-visible remembered evidence
-> relationship qualifies learning
-> permanent Trait owns durable capability
-> capability changes intentional choices
-> repeated understood work becomes delegatable
-> player attention returns to novel / meaningful choices
```

Relationship explains learning provenance. Trait state owns durable capability. Gameplay determines local applicability. The player chooses whether to use a capability or delegate an eligible routine.

## Repository evidence

The repository already demonstrates the relationship chain from Experience and Memory through Trait assimilation and permanent capability, including `ScholarlyInsight` in more than one gameplay domain. It also demonstrates persistent routine familiarity, bounded Copy eligibility, player-authored routine priority, and explicit delegation. Player Insight and contextual dialogue presentation already provide read-only causal surfaces.

The selected synthesis therefore deepens existing evidence rather than inventing a new product center.

## Counter-evidence and uncertainty

The repository does not establish that players perceive relationships as buildcraft, value capability provenance, enjoy causal explanation, find delegation satisfying, prefer this identity over alternatives, or experience good pacing, fairness, fun, or retention. Those claims remain unproven and may later reverse this priority order.

## Provisionally authorized depth

M26 may deepen only the following bounded surfaces:

- **Capability provenance:** connect discovered/permanent relationship-derived capabilities to already-player-visible Memories that contributed to assimilation.
- **Mastery provenance:** explain which repeatable routines the player personally mastered and which recorded source created familiarity.
- **Delegation legibility:** distinguish player mastery from Copy-specific readiness while preserving existing M20 eligibility authority.
- **Integrated qualification:** prove the above remain read-only projections over canonical state.
