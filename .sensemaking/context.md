# Context

## Terms that are easy to confuse
- **Affinity is not Connection XP.** Affinity does not award progression; relationship history does. [policy: specification/Technical/PostM14ProductReconciliation.md]
- **Relationship Experience vs. Memory.** Experiences are recorded events; Memories form only from landmark Experiences. [policy: specification/GameDesignDocument.md]
- **Bond Profile / Connection** is accumulated evidence, not a single meter. [policy: specification/GameDesignDocument.md]
- **Essence** is passive metaphysical resource generation. It is not the same as Knowledge. [policy: specification/GameDesignDocument.md]
- **Knowledge (per NPC)** can diverge from objective facts and transfers explicitly. [policy: specification/GameDesignDocument.md]
- **Faction Reputation** is institutional standing, distinct from personal Relationship and Knowledge. [policy: specification/GameDesignDocument.md]
- **Objective World State** is distinct from social interpretation of events. [policy: specification/GameDesignDocument.md]
- **Copy** is a delegated agent that applies only player-authorized, understood routine work. It does not plan autonomously. [policy: specification/Features/FeatureScopeMatrix.md]
- **Evidence classes** (DETERMINISTIC, HEURISTIC, SYNTHETIC, HUMAN) are not interchangeable. Synthetic results are never human evidence. [policy: AGENTS.md]
- **FEATURE_COMPLETE** is recorded, human-unvalidated. It does not mean polished or released. [policy: specification/Technical/FeatureCompleteResult.md]
