# 0002: Synthetic findings never count as human evidence

**Decision:** Automated, model-assisted, and simulated results may justify bounded repairs but do not support claims about what players understood or enjoyed.  
**Alternatives rejected:** treating a passing playthrough as player evidence.  
**Why:** [policy: AGENTS.md] "Evidence classes" section.  
**Revisit when:** the owner changes the Beta evidence procedure. (open: owner decision)
