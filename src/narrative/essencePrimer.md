# Essence Primer (Phase 3)

Essence is latent emotional / cognitive potential made legible through connection.

## Player-Facing Short Definition
Essence: Potential crystallized from meaningful rapport. Spend it to shape who you can become.

## Systems Framing
- Generation Sources: passive tick (baseline), relationship actions (future), quest outcomes (future), trait modifiers.
- Modifiers: Multiplicative stack (e.g., EssenceFlow, MentorsInsight) applied after base computation.
- Expenditure: Unlock / purchase traits, later: ritual events, resonance amplifiers.
